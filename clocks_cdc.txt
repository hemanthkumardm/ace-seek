• CDC guide = data paths between those objects when they are async.

Practical rule

1. One clock or related clocks (div/PLL) → start with CLOCKS_COMPLETE_USER_GUIDE.md.
2. Two+ unrelated clocks and data between them → CDC_USER_GUIDE.md, and use the clocks guide when you need generated clocks or full set_clock_groups options.

They are complements, not duplicates. After the merge, CDC is one file; clocks stay separate because generated clocks / latency / duty apply even when you have no CDC.
